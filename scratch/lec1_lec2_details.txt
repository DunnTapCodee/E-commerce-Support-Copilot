=== Page 18: World, machine, and the statements ===
World, machine, and the statements 
between them
• Requirements (REQ) are expressed only in terms of world phenomena
• Assumptions (ASM) expressed in terms of world & shared phenomena
• Specifications (SPEC) are expressed in terms of shared phenomena
• Key point: Software cannot directly satisfy a requirement on its own; it 
relies on assumptions about the environment!
18


=== Page 19: Important to make assumptions explicit ===
Important to make assumptions explicit
• System Requirement (REQ): What the system must ensure, in terms 
of desired effects on the environment
• Software Specification (SPEC): What software must implement, 
expressed over the shared phenomena (input/output devices)
• Assumptions (ASM): What’s assumed about the behavior/properties 
of the environment; bridges the gap between REQ and SPEC
ASM ∧ SPEC => REQ

=== Page 41: Bounded purpose and system scope ===

=== Page 42: Two measurable requirements ===
Two measurable requirements
• REQ-01 - Correct, grounded guidance
• For in-scope undergraduate graduation questions, at least 95% of 
answers on a versioned, stratified evaluation set shall provide the 
correct eligibility guidance and cite the applicable current policy; 
performance shall not fall below 90% for any monitored program or 
entry-year slice.
• REQ-02 - Safe handling of uncertainty
• When required records are missing, policies conflict, or an exception is 
needed, the system shall abstain and create an adviser-review case in 
at least 99% of such cases, rather than provide a definitive eligibility 
judgment. The case shall include the student’s question, relevant 
record fields, retrieved policy passages, and the reason for escalation.

Two critical assumptions
• ASM-01 - Policy freshness
• The registrar publishes every approved policy change in an 
authoritative digital source with an effective date before the policy is 
applied to students.
• ASM-02 - Human-service capacity
• Academic advising has sufficient staffing and authority to review 
escalated graduation cases within the promised service window, 
including during peak registration and graduation periods.

=== Page 45: Owner and unresolved evidence need ===
Owner and unresolved evidence need
• Accountable owner:
• UniGuide Service Owner: accountable for verifying that the complete 
prevention-detection-response-recovery chain operates across the 
registrar, engineering, and advising teams.
• Unresolved evidence need:
• We do not yet know whether the advising service can process the 
projected number of escalations within the promised response time 
during peak periods. This must be measured through workload data or 
a controlled pilot before confirming ASM-02 or finalizing the release 
scope.
